Save contact
  1. Gmail: Settings (gear) > See all settings > General > Signature > Create new. Paste, then Save Changes at the bottom.
  2. New Outlook / Outlook on the web: Settings > Accounts > Signatures. Paste and save.
  3. Classic Outlook (desktop): File > Options > Mail > Signatures > New. Paste and save.
